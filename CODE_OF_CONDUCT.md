# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions. Welcome contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds. Critique ideas and code, and give others room to clarify mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately using the maintainer's contact options on [GitHub](https://github.com/MuhammadRaedSiddiqui). Do not include private details in public issues. Maintainers should treat reports confidentially and apply these expectations consistently, including to themselves.
